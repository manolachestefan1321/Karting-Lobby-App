{{APP_NAME}} ("we", "us") is operated by {{LEGAL_ENTITY}}, {{LEGAL_ADDRESS}}. This policy explains what personal data we process when you use the {{APP_NAME}} mobile app and the {{APP_NAME}} website, and how you can control it. We act as data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Data we collect
- **Account data:** email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), sign-in provider (Apple, Google, Facebook) and the basic profile data that provider shares with us (name, email).
- **Profile data:** first and last name, date of birth (others see only your age), home city, short bio, profile photo, karting experience per track.
- **Activity data:** sessions you create or join, lobby chat messages, check-ins, results, points, rank, no-shows and bans, reports you send or receive.
- **Location:** only at check-in time, and only to confirm that you are within the configured distance of the track. We do not track your location in the background.
- **Payment data:** payments are processed by Stripe. We receive the amount, status and a payment reference, but never your full card number.
- **Device data:** push-notification token, app version, language.
- **Launch waitlist (website):** first name, last name, phone number and email you enter in the "Join the race" form, and the date you signed up.
- **Website statistics (only if you accept them):** pages visited, how you arrived (for example an Instagram link), device type (phone, tablet or computer) and visit times, linked to a random visitor ID. No name, email or IP address is stored with them.

## 2. Why we use it (legal basis)
- To provide the service: accounts, lobbies, payments, refunds, rankings (contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- To keep the community fair and safe: no-show penalties, moderation of reports (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).
- To meet legal obligations: accounting and tax records of payments (Art. 6(1)(c)).
- To send notifications you can switch off in Settings (consent / legitimate interest).
- To contact you about the launch event after you join the waitlist (consent, Art. 6(1)(a)).
- To count visits and improve the website, only if you accept statistics cookies (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, as implemented by Romanian Law 506/2004). You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.

## 3. Who sees your data
- **Other users** see your public profile: photo, name, age, city, bio, rank, points, sessions and tracks raced, and an active ban if you have one. Lobby members see lobby chat messages.
- **Service providers** acting on our instructions: Supabase (hosting, EU region), Stripe (payments), Expo (push delivery), our email provider.
- **Hosts** of a session receive payouts through Stripe; they do not receive your payment details.
- **The track where you book a seat** sees your name and your booking code (KUP-…) in its KartUP desk panel, so it can check you in and count the drivers KartUP brings. It does not see your email, phone number or profile details.
- We never sell your data.

## 4. How long we keep it
Account and profile data: until you delete your account. Payment and refund records: as long as Romanian accounting law requires (currently up to 10 years), without your name after account deletion. Chat messages: until the session is 12 months old. Launch waitlist: until 12 months after the launch event, or earlier if you ask us to delete it. Website statistics: 13 months. Records of your cookie choice: 3 years, to prove consent.

## 5. Cookies and local storage (website)
We store a few small items in your browser. We do **not** use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kartup-consent` | strictly necessary | remembers your cookie choice | 6 months, then we ask again |
| `kartup-preferences` | strictly necessary | language and light/dark theme you chose | until you clear it |
| `sb-…-auth-token` | strictly necessary | keeps you signed in (Supabase) | until you sign out |
| `kartup-vid` | statistics (only with consent) | random visitor ID, to count unique visitors | 13 months, deleted when you withdraw consent |
| `kartup-sid` | statistics (only with consent) | random visit ID, to count visits | until you close the tab |

Strictly necessary items don't need consent (Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive). Statistics are switched **off** until you accept them, and the website works the same if you refuse. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of the website or in Settings.
